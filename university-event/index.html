About BUP TechFest

🚀

BUP TechFest 2026 is a student-focused technology festival organized to encourage innovation, technical knowledge and practical problem solving.

The festival will bring together students, technology enthusiasts, researchers and industry professionals through robotics competitions, programming contests, project exhibitions and technical sessions.

Participants will have the opportunity to showcase their projects, compete with other teams and learn about emerging technologies.
